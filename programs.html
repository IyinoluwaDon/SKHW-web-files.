Programs

How It Works

One day to spark it, one mentorship to sustain it. Built to reach every girl in the room and stay with her long after we leave.

The engagement model

One session, then a mentorship

A single afternoon can plant something powerful, but it takes follow-through to help it grow. So every cohort begins with one session on the day, and a week later, the mentorship programme begins to build on it.

The Day

One Session, One Room

Everything happens in a single session. It opens with a panel, where our speakers each give a talk drawn from real experience, speaking to the girls directly and honestly. Then we step back and explain why this initiative exists, and bring its purpose to life in the room, so every girl leaves knowing what She Knows Her Worth stands for and that it was built for her.

  • A panel session with our guest speakers
  • Talks from each speaker, in their own voice
  • The purpose of the initiative, clearly explained
  • That purpose brought to life, with the girls at the centre
One Week Later

The Mentorship Programme

A week after the session, the mentorship programme launches. It is a hands-on training experience where mentors sit with the girls, talk with them, and teach them. Everything follows a structured syllabus, so growth is planned, steady, and built to last well beyond the day we first met.

  • A structured syllabus, taught step by step
  • Upskilling in leadership and public speaking
  • Writing and other practical skills for life and school
  • Open conversations and direct guidance from mentors

Woven through everything we do

The three pillars, in the room

Menstrual Dignity

Hygiene education and materials that keep girls in school and in control of their bodies.

Identity & Empowerment

Mentorship that rewrites the story a girl tells herself about her own worth.

Safety & Emotional Well-being

Consent education, abuse awareness, and real referral pathways when it’s needed.
